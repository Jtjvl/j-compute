Services

Concrete help for faculty, research IT, and department admins who need reliable compute and systems.

  • Scientific computing

    HPC-minded workflows, numerical pipelines, data handling, and performance work for research groups.

  • Systems architecture

    Clear designs for lab and department systems — storage, identity, networking, and service boundaries that scale with the group.

  • Custom programming

    Glue code, tooling, integrations, and application work when off-the-shelf stacks stop fitting the research.

  • University & research IT

    Practical support alongside campus IT: pilots, migrations, documentation, and handoff that departments can own.

Approach

  1. Scope the real problem

    Short discovery with the people who run the systems day to day — constraints, compliance, and what “done” looks like.

  2. Design and build

    Architecture plus implementation. You get working systems and diagrams, not slide decks alone.

  3. Hand off cleanly

    Runbooks, ownership, and knowledge transfer so the department isn’t stuck with a black box.
